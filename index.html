About me

I'm a final-year BSc Information Technology student who learns by building real things.

I started coding in 2019 because I was curious how apps and websites actually get made. Turning an idea into something that really works is still the part I enjoy most. What began as curiosity became a love of solving problems and learning new technologies, and I've been building ever since.

Away from the screen, I'm usually listening to music, training at the gym, playing football or gaming, or travelling when I get the chance. I'm also into business and entrepreneurship, and I like spending time with friends. Keeping a balance between all of that is how I stay sharp for the work.
